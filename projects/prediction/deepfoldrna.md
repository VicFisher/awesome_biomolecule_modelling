## Key innovation
An **automated RNA 3D structure prediction** method that scored **1st** in an independent
systematic benchmark of deep-learning RNA folders (PLOS Comp Biol 2024), ahead of DRFold, RhoFold+,
and RoseTTAFoldNA.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- **RNA-only**; benchmark rank is dataset-dependent.
- Still modest absolute accuracy on novel/large RNAs.

## Benchmarks
- Best-performing automated RNA folder in the PLOS Comp Biol systematic evaluation (2024).
