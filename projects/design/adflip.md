## Key innovation
A **discrete flow-matching** model for **all-atom inverse folding** — designing sequences
conditioned on full all-atom context including ligands. Reports recent state-of-the-art on both
single-state and multi-state inverse folding.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Very new (low citation base); limited independent validation.
- All-atom + flow-matching is compute-heavy.
- Multi-state design requires multiple input backbone states.

## Benchmarks
- Reported SOTA on single- and multi-state inverse-folding benchmarks. (Source: ADFLIP, ICML 2025.)
