## Key innovation
A generative model using **partially latent flow matching** for de novo design of **protein binders
and enzymes** — a flow-matching alternative to the diffusion paradigm (RFdiffusion/BoltzGen).

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Very recent; no peer-reviewed paper yet (technical release).
- Commercial (NVIDIA); limited independent validation.
- Inherits the general gap between in-silico designability and experimental success.
