## Key innovation
Next-generation **all-atom diffusion** model extending RFdiffusionAA to design binders/enzymes
against **DNA, RNA, and broader biomolecular targets**. Pushes all-atom generative design beyond
protein-protein/small-molecule to nucleic-acid-binding proteins.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Very recent preprint; limited independent validation.
- Inherits diffusion-design needs for screening + affinity maturation.
- Compute-heavy all-atom diffusion.

## Benchmarks
- Reported de novo binders/enzymes against nucleic-acid and other targets (per preprint).
  (Source: RFdiffusion3, bioRxiv 2025.)
