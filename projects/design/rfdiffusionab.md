## Key innovation
Adapts **RFdiffusion to de novo antibody design** — generating full antibody binders (framework +
CDRs) to a target antigen, with designs validated by **cryo-EM**. A milestone for generative
antibody design (vs. earlier CDR-only methods like DiffAb/dyMEAN).

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Very recent; binders still typically require affinity maturation / experimental optimization.
- Antibody-specific; compute-heavy diffusion.
- Limited independent validation beyond the authors'.

## Benchmarks
- Cryo-EM-validated de novo antibodies against target antigens (per paper). (Source: RFdiffusionAb,
  Nature 2025.)
