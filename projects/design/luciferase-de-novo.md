## Key innovation
A landmark **de novo enzyme design** result: generated novel **luciferase** enzymes (catalyzing
light emission) from scratch using RFdiffusion-family scaffolding + ProteinMPNN + high-throughput
screening, achieving high substrate specificity and activity — the first practically useful de novo
luciferases. A milestone application of generative design for catalysis.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- **Application/milestone**, not a new algorithm (uses RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN + screening).
- Required large high-throughput screening to find active designs.
- Substrate-specific; generalizing to other enzymes needs case-by-case work.

## Benchmarks
- Multiple novel, soluble, specific luciferases experimentally validated. (Source: Yeh et al., Nature
  2023.)
