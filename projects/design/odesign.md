## Key innovation
An **all-atom generative "world model" for all-to-all biomolecular interaction design** — specify a
biomolecular target and generate binding partners across molecule types (proteins, nucleic acids,
small molecules). Frames interaction design as a world-modeling (dynamics-style) generation problem
rather than single-target binder design.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Very recent (Oct 2025); no public repo/citation record yet.
- Limited independent validation; claims await community confirmation.
- Cross-molecule-type generation is ambitious and likely uneven across modalities.

## Benchmarks
- All-to-all biomolecular interaction design across molecule types (per preprint). (Source: ODesign,
