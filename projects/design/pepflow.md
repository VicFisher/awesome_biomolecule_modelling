## Key innovation
The first **multi-modal deep generative model grounded in flow matching** for **full-atom peptide
design** — extending the flow-matching paradigm (cf. FoldFlow/FrameFlow) to peptide sequence +
structure co-generation.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- **Peptide-only**; very recent.
- Needs binding-site/target context; limited experimental validation.

## Benchmarks
- Full-atom peptide generation via flow matching vs diffusion baselines. (Source: PepFlow, 2024.)
