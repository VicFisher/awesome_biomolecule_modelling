## Key innovation
A **score-based SE(3)-equivariant diffusion model over backbone frames** that emphasizes
**designable, novel, and diverse** generated structures — an early equivariant backbone diffusion
contemporary with FrameDiff.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- **Backbone-only**; less adopted than RFdiffusion/Chroma.
- Needs separate sequence design + validation.
- Newer/less validated than the Baker-lab diffusion ecosystem.

## Benchmarks
- Reported designable/novel/diverse structure generation vs baselines. (Source: Genie, arXiv
  2301.12485.)
