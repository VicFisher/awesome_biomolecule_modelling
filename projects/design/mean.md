## Key innovation
A **multi-channel E(3)-equivariant attention network** that **co-designs antibody CDR sequence and
3D structure** via conditional graph translation — an early equivariant antibody co-design model and
the seed of dyMEAN.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- **Antibody-only**; CDR-focused.
- Older; superseded by dyMEAN and diffusion-based antibody design (DiffAb, RFdiffusionAb).
- Needs antigen structure for conditional design.

## Benchmarks
- Antibody CDR sequence-structure co-design vs prior baselines. (Source: MEAN, NeurIPS 2022.)
