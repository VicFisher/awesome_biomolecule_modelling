## Key innovation
**RFdiffusion2 generates enzyme backbones with custom active sites directly from reaction
specifications** — pushing de novo enzyme design to atomic-level control of catalytic geometry.
Extends the RFdiffusion family to reaction-conditioned enzyme scaffolding.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Very recent preprint; needs independent/experimental validation.
- Enzyme design still requires high-throughput screening for activity.
- Compute-heavy; sensitive to active-site theozyme input.

## Benchmarks
- Atomic-level active-site scaffolding from reaction specs (per preprint). (Source: Cao et al.,
  bioRxiv 2025.)
