## Key innovation
A dataset of **62k DFT relaxations (~9.85M single-point calculations)** of oxide electrocatalysts, capturing adsorption energies on metal-oxide surfaces and the **oxygen-evolution reaction (OER) scaling relations** central to electrocatalysis. OC22 extends the Open Catalyst effort from metallic surfaces to **oxides**, which dominate real electrocatalyst chemistry but are harder for DFT and for learned potentials.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- **Oxide-electrocatalysis focus** — not biomolecular.
- Smaller than OC20; requires careful task setup (binary oxide vs. peroxide classification).
- Magnetic/oxidation-state complexity in oxides challenges universal MLPs.

## Benchmarks
Tasks: adsorption-energy MAE and peroxide/binary-oxide classification; OER-scaling-relation fidelity. GemNet, SCN, and later models report on OC22 and on OC20+OC22 joint training. (Source: Tran et al., ACS Catal. 2022 / NeurIPS D&B 2022.)
