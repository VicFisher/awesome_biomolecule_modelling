## Key innovation
A dataset of **130M+ DFT relaxations** of adsorbates on surfaces (across 55 elements and dozens of materials) for heterogeneous-catalysis discovery, paired with **standardized tasks** IS2RS (initial structure → relaxed structure), IS2RE (initial → relaxed energy), and S2EF (structure → energy/forces). OC20 **scaled the field to catalyst-sized datasets** and launched community challenges that became the de-facto benchmarks for large-scale equivariant GNNs (GemNet, SCN, eGNNO, eqV2).

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Focus is **surface catalysis** — not biomolecular.
- A **single DFT level** (mostly RPBE); relaxations rather than full MD trajectories.
- Benchmark has partially saturated over time; the very largest models approach the task ceiling.

## Benchmarks
Tasks: **IS2RE** energy MAE, **IS2RS** top-k structure accuracy, **S2EF** energy/force MAE. GemNet, SCN, EquiformerV2/eqV2 report steady improvements on the public leaderboard. (Source: Chanussot et al., ACS Catal. 2021.)
