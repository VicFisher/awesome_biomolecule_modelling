## Key innovation
A **foundation MLIP trained jointly across multiple molecular domains and elements**, learning a
universal atomistic representation for broad generalization. Cited as a leading foundation-model
potential alongside MACE, ORB, MatterSim, and EquiformerV2.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Large-scale/commercial-style training; closed pipeline.
- Breadth trades off against per-system accuracy.
- Limited independent biomolecular benchmarking.

## Benchmarks
- Broad transfer across chemistry (per project claims); independent benchmarks pending.
