## Key innovation
A benchmark of **seven biomolecular and supramolecular systems (42–370 atoms)** — including an Ac-Ala-NHMe helix, a tripeptide (Gly-Gly-Gly), nucleotides (ATP, Resorcinarene), a docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) chain, a stearic acid, and C60 fullerene. MD22 probes **non-local and many-body effects beyond MD17's small molecules**, asking whether NNPs scale to hundreds of atoms with significant flexibility. Released with sGDML, it became the standard "large molecule" companion to MD17/rMD17.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Only **seven systems**; breadth of chemistry is limited.
- Still single-trajectory sampling per system; DFT level fixed (PBE+MBD).
- Forces on large floppy molecules remain genuinely hard — error floors are higher than MD17.

## Benchmarks
Task: **energy/force RMSE** at ~1k–10k training frames. sGDML, GemNet, PaiNN, and MACE report here; modern equivariant models substantially improve over kernel methods on the largest systems (ATP, fullerene). (Source: Chmiela et al., Sci. Adv. 2023 / arXiv 2209.14865.)
