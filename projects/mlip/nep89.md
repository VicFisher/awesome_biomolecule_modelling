## Key innovation
A **universal neuroevolution potential (NEP)** trained across inorganic and organic chemistry,
achieving **empirical-potential-like speed** while retaining near-DFT accuracy across both regimes.

## Limitations / disadvantages
- Uses the **NEP framework** (specific ecosystem/toolchain).
- Very recent; limited independent validation vs MACE/SevenNet on biomolecular tasks.
- Neuroevolution training is unusual; less interoperable than PyTorch GNN potentials.

## Benchmarks
- Reported empirical-potential-speed inference across organic + inorganic systems (per preprint).
  (Source: NEP89, arXiv 2504.21286.)
